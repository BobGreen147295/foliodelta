# Recipient outcome

One fresh AI agent received the [fictional packet](packet) and an English finance request asking whether refunds explained the lower later export and whether Finance could close the case. The task explicitly requested an independent source check, original order status and a ready-to-forward English reply. The facilitator withheld the expected answer and the scoring rubric from the assigned inputs. The original agent reply was preserved before requesting a single follow-up.

| Predefined check | First reply | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Locate first order and verify absence | Partial | Named CASE-542, its side and amount. The reply omitted its source line. |
| Recompute order totals | Met | 184.40 to 154.90 USD. Signed contributions of −52.00, +35.00 and −12.50 sum to −29.50. Counted the additional line item once at the order level. |
| Separate recorded refund change and verify original status | Partial | Recovered the unchanged partially_refunded status from both CSVs and identified the +8.25 change on CASE-543. Both line references were missing. |
| Decide what can close | Met | Signed off the arithmetic. Requested export settings, order history and payment records before signing off the cause. |
| Specify ownership and next evidence | Met | Order Operations to trace one-sided records and the edit; Payments to check transactions by order, amount, currency and date. |

First reply: three criteria met fully and two partially. After one request for source locations, the recipient supplied all three expected line references and described the absence check. The facilitator independently verified the line contents, recomputed totals from the raw CSVs and regenerated the handoff. Follow-up performance is reported separately from first-attempt performance.

**Product implication.** The CLI handoff already contains source lines, yet they disappeared when the receiving agent condensed the report into an email. A useful next test would ask another recipient to preserve a compact filename-and-line reference alongside each decision and measure whether Finance can locate it without a follow-up. No external message, store check, processor lookup or real merchant action occurred.
